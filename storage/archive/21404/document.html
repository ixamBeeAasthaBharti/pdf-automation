  • 
    The tax that is levied by the government directly on the individuals or corporations are called
    Direct Taxes.

Direct & Indirect Tax
2
  • 
    An indirect tax is a tax that is collected through a middleman from the person who suffers
    the tax's ultimate economic burden.
  • 
    It is possible for the taxpayer to transfer it to someone else.
  • 
    The intermediary prepares a tax return and sends the tax proceeds to the government along
    with it.
  • 
    Indirect taxes are based on an individual's expenses rather than their income.
  • 
    Indirect taxes are levied on suppliers of goods and services, but the tax is passed on to the
    consumers, who are indirectly paying the tax.
  • 
    Some examples of Indirect Taxes are- Customs Duty and Goods & Services Tax (GST).

Merits

1)Convenient to pay and less cumbersome

2)Large revenue Potential

3)Important for influencing pattern of production and investment

Demerits

1)Indirect taxes are regressive

2)Indirect taxes are inflationary

3)Excess burden of indirect taxes or inefficiency of resource allocation
