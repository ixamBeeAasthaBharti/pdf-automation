Industrial Policy

Introduction

Industrial Policy is the procedures, principles, policies, rules and regulations which control
the industries of the country and it also determines the pattern of industrialization. It also
refers to Government action to influence the ownership & structure of the industry and its
performance. It takes the form of pay-ing subsidies or providing finance in other ways, or of
regulation.

It includes procedures, principles (i.e., the philosophy of a given economy), policies, rules
and
regulations,
in-centives
and
punishments,
the
tariff
policy,
the
labour
policy,
government’s attitude towards foreign capital, etc.

Indian Industrial Policy

Industrial Policy Resolution of 1948

  • 
    The first industrial policy after independence was announced on 6th April 1948.
  • 
    This policy aimed to accelerate the industrial development by introducing a mixed economy
    where the private and public sector was accepted as important in the development of the
    economy.
  • 
    It set Indian economy in socialistic patterns.
  • 
    The large industries were classified into four categories under this policy statement

  • o
    Industries with exclusive State Monopoly (Strategic industries) -These included
    industries engaged in the activity of atomic energy, railways and arms and ammunition.
  • o
    Industries with Government control-This category included industries which are of
    national importance. 18 such categories were mentioned such as fertilizers, heavy
    machinery, defense equipment, heavy chemicals, etc.
  • o
    Industries with Mixed sector-This category included industries that were allowed to
    operate independently in private or in public sector. The government was allowed to
    review the situation to acquire any existing private undertaking in such category.
