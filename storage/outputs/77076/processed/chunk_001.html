Direction (1-4)

Eight friends namely J, R, S, P, A, N, D and T consist of a family of three generations such that there are two married couples in the family. For every person of the family either both or none of the parents are alive. S's sister-in-law is mother of D. T is an unmarried female. D's mother doesn't have any siblings. J is the father-in-law of N who is the mother of two children. R is grandmother of D who is nephew of P.

Solution

1. How many children does J. have?

  • (a) One
  • (b) Two
  • (c) Three
  • (d) Four
  • (e) Five

Ans: (c)

2. How is A related to T?

  • (a) Mother
  • (b) Brother
  • (c) Aunt
  • (d) Uncle
  • (e) Father

Ans: (e)

3. How is S related to R?

  • (a) Daughter
  • (b) Son in law
  • (c) Son
  • (d) Husband
  • (e) CND

Ans: (e)

4. How is N related to P?

  • (a) Sister-in-law
  • (b) Mother-in-law
  • (c) Wife
  • (d) Daughter in law
  • (e) Brother-in-law

Ans: (a)
