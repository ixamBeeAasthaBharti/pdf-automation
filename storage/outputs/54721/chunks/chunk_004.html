Section 276. Removal and replacement of liquidator: (1) The Tribunal may, on a
reasonable cause being shown and for reasons to be recorded in writing, remove the
provisional liquidator or the Company Liquidator, as the case may be, as liquidator of the

during the term of his appointment that would justify removal.
