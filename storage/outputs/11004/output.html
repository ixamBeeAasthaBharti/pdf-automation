Fisheries Statistics 2023

Fisheries Statistics 2022-23

  1. Total fish production is 175.45 lakh tonnes, with marine fish production of 44.32 lakh tonnes and inland fish production of 131.13 lakh tonnes for the year 2022-23.
  2. 17,35,286 tonnes of fisheries export valued at INR 63,969.14 crore, recorded for year 2022-23 as compared to 13,69,264 tonnes of fisheries export valued at INR 57,586.48 crore in 2021-2022.
  3. Gross Value Added (GVA) by the Fisheries Sector increased to INR 2,88,526 crore in 2021-22, from Gross Value Addition of INR 252,512 in the year 2020-21.
  4. India's contribution to the World Fish Production reached 8.92% in 2022-23
  5. The total number of Fish Landing Centres across the Country are 1,547.
  1. There are seven major Fishing Harbours, and Sixty-Three minor Fishing Harbours that have been commissioned.
  2. A total of 1,66,052 Kisan Credit Cards (KCC) for fishers were sanctioned as on 27th October 2023.
  3. In direction of the future, the integration of cage culture and RAS in fisheries provide several benefits such as water quality management, intensification of production, etc through PMMSY scheme.
