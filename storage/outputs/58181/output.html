Exam Pattern

The Recruitment Test will be used to shortlist candidates for interview. The weightage in the ratio of 75:25 is accorded for marks in Recruitment Test and for marks in interview in determining final merit.

  • Mode – Offline Pen and Paper based Test
  • Duration – 2 hours
  • Type – Objective, MCQ based
  • Medium – English Only
  • Marks – maximum of 300 marks; all questions will carry equal marks (No. of Qs is not mentioned; however, based on Recruitment Test of 2023, 120 questions will be asked of 2.5 marks each)
  • Penalty for wrong answers - Every wrong answer will carry a deduction of one-third of the marks assigned to that question. If no answer is marked for a question, there will be no penalty for that question.

Interview stage

Standard of suitability for the interview for candidates belonging to various categories is fixed as under:

  • General/EWS Category: 50 and above
  • OBC: 45 and above (on relaxed standards)
  • SC/ST: 40 and above (on relaxed standards)
  • PWD (Gen/EWS/OBC/SC/ST): 40 and above (on relaxed standards)

  • The Study Plan given below focuses on the Recruitment Test
  • Time commitment: 7-8 hours daily (This may vary as per your preparation level and the score in Mock Tests)
  • You should write Section Test or Mock test regularly for revision. Analyse the tests you have attempted, and first focus on the questions which you answered incorrectly and then focus on all un-attempted questions - in each section.
