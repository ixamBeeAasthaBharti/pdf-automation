Maternity Benefit Act 1961  
Introduction  

The Maternity Benefit Act regulate the employment of women in certain establishments  
for certain periods before and after child-birth and to provide for maternity benefit and  
certain other benefits.  


It extends to whole of India.  
It applies to  
 every establishment being a factory, mine or plantation including any such  
establishment belonging to Government and to every establishment wherein  
persons are employed for the exhibition of equestrian, acrobatic and other  
performances  
 every shop or establishment within the meaning of any law for the time being in  
force in relation to shops and establishments in a State, in which ten or more  
persons are employed, or were employed, on any day of the preceding twelve  
months  

State Government can declare that the provisions of this Act shall apply also to any other  
establishment or class of establishments, industrial, commercial, agricultural or otherwise,  
after giving a minimum 2 month’s notice, with the prior approval of Central  
Government.


Every woman shall be entitled to, and her employer shall be liable for, the payment  
of maternity benefit at the rate of the average daily wage for the period of her actual  
absence, that is to say, the period immediately preceding the day of her delivery,  
the actual day of her delivery and any period immediately following that day.  
A woman is entitled to maternity benefit if she has actually worked in an  
establishment for a period not less than 80 days in 12 months immediately  
preceding the date of her expected delivery.  
The maximum period of maternity benefit shall be 26 weeks of which not more than  
8 weeks shall precede the date of her expected delivery. If woman has 2 or more  
surviving children, maternity benefit will be for 12 weeks, of which not more than 6  
weeks shall precede the date of expected delivery. If a woman dies, maternity  
benefit shall be payable only for the days up to and including the day of her death  
Legal adoption of a child below the age of 3 months or a commissioning mother  
shall be e entitled to maternity benefit for a period of twelve weeks from the date  
the child is handed over to the adopting mother or the commissioning mother
