IBPS RRB Sc II Law – Interview Guide  
Important interview questions  
Your Profile related  
1. Tell us something about yourself/Tell us something that is not in your bio-data/ Introduce  
yourself/ Can you run us through your bio-data?  
Very Important Question  
If this is the first question, then your whole interview will be based on answer to this  
question.  
Prepare for an answer of at least 30 seconds and Maximum 1 minute.  
2. How would your friends describe you? / If you have to define yourself in 3 words, what will be  
those three words  
3. Do your SWOT analysis/What is your biggest strength and weakness? What sets you apart  
from other candidates? / Your strength / Why should we select you? What do you bring on  
table for RBI?  
Prepare for at least 2 strengths and 2 weakness. Have examples to substantiate each.  
Weakness should be something that does not hamper your prospects as a prospective  
employee  
4. Your biggest achievement till date  
Prepare for at least 2. It can be academic/professional or non-academic also  
5. How do you look at failure? Your biggest failure till date/ What is Success?  
6. What do you do in your leisure time? Your interests and hobbies? What co-curriculum  
activities have you participated in?  
7. What is your town/city/state famous for?  
8. What does your name mean?/ Do you truly represent your name? (in case you have a unique  
name/famous name, know the meaning of the name/any famous personalities by your name)  
9. Who is your role model and why? Who do you admire and why?  
10. Who or what motivates you?  
11. Which is more important to you: money or the type of job?  
12. What are the three most important events of your life?/What was an experience in your life  
that you would want to go back and change?

IBPS RRB Sc II Law – Interview Guide  
Important interview questions for Legal  
Indicative list only:  
1. Financial markets related terms - Repo rate, reverse repo rate, SLR, CRR, NPA, NIM,  
SMA etc  
2. Major functions of a Bank  
3. How to identify a fake currency note  
4. Difference between current account and saving account  
5. What is the difference between pledge, mortgage and hypothecation  
6. Corporate Governance  
7. What is meant by ‘absolute liability’ under Section 138 of NI Act?  
8. Which all instruments are mentioned in NI Act?  
9. Difference between promissory note, bill of exchange, cheque.  
10. Any recent amendment in  
11. Banking Laws  
12. Which all laws will be used in the job profile of a law officer in Regional Rural bank?  
13. Difference between Securitization and Asset Reconstruction under SARFAESI Act  
14. Procedure of Enforcement of Security Interest under SARFAESI  
15. Is there a provision under SARFAESI to go to DRT?  
16. Contract Act - Offer, Acceptance etc  
17. Related Party Transactions18.  
18. Writs  
19. Key provisions of FERA/FEMA/Banking Regulation Act/RBI Act etc  
20. Principle of Oppression and Management under Company Law  
21. Any recent case law  
22. Name of Supreme Court Judges  
23. Judicial review vs Judicial Activism  
24. What is natural justice?  
25. Delegated legislation  
26. Indemnity vs Guarantee  
27. Examples of these in bank  
28. Continuing Guarantee  
29. Doctrine of eclipse, severability  
30. Contempt of Court  
31. Court of Record  
32. Legal current affairs  
Please note, that the ‘Legal’ interview can go in any direction; towards any act and would  
progress based on your replies to the previous questions and also based on what experience  
you have. This is not an exhaustive list and is indicative only. Questions are inclined towards  
the understanding of various acts, their applicability and interlinkages.
